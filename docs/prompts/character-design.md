# Character design prompt

Image-generation prompt for designing a new Bizarre Chess character. Fill in the `[brackets]`, and attach two or three existing portraits from `public/assets/characters/<name>/` (e.g. `ren/portrait.jpg`, `mio/portrait.jpg`) so the style matches the cast.

For the portrait the game actually uses, generate item 1 on its own as a square image with nothing else in it.

---

Character design sheet for "Bizarre Chess", an anime chess-duel game. Match the art style of the attached reference portraits exactly: soft painterly anime illustration, delicate linework, pale skin with a gentle blush, expressive eyes, school-uniform-inspired outfits (white collared shirt, dark tie or ribbon, dark jacket), and swirling ink or smoke in the character's signature colour on a light textured grey background.

Character: [name], [age, around 17–20], [gender].
Personality: [two or three words, e.g. "cold, proud, secretly lonely"].
Role in the tournament: [rival / antagonist / ally], known for [their play style, e.g. "reckless sacrifices"].
Signature colour: [colour name + hex, e.g. "teal #2ad4c0"], used for their ink swirls, eye glow and accents.
Theme / power: [e.g. "storms and lightning", "mirrors", "candles and wax"], hinted at in their accessories and the swirls around them.
Look: [hair colour and style], [eye colour], [one distinctive feature, e.g. "a bandage on the cheek", "a cracked mask", "gloves", "an earring shaped like a chess piece"], [how they wear the uniform, e.g. "loose tie, sleeves rolled up"].
Holding: a chess piece that suits them, a [king / queen / knight…], in the style of their theme.

Sheet layout, all on the same light grey background:

1. Main portrait, square, chest-up, a three-quarter view, holding their chess piece near the face, signature-colour ink swirling around them. (This is the one used on cards and in battle.)
2. Full-body standing pose, front view, confident stance.
3. Five small expression heads: calm, smug/happy, angry, shocked, defeated.
4. Their chess piece in close-up, plus their signature accessory.

No text, no labels, no watermark.
