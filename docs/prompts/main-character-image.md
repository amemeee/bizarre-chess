# Main character image prompt

Image-generation prompt for a character's **main portrait**: the square image used on cards, in battle, story scenes and cut-ins (`public/assets/characters/<name>/portrait.jpg`). It copies the crop, pose and ink aura of `characters/ren/portrait.jpg` so every portrait in the cast lines up.

**Attach two images, in this order:**
1. The character's design (their design sheet, or an older portrait).
2. `public/assets/characters/ren/portrait.jpg`, the template for the crop, pose and ink aura.

Fill in the `[brackets]`. A filled-in example (AI-0) is at the bottom.

---

**Character portrait for "Bizarre Chess".** Use the **first attached image** for the character. Use the **second attached image (Ren) as an exact template for the crop, pose, ink-aura pattern and painting style**, but not for the face, hair or outfit.

**Character (from the first image):** [name], [one line on who they are]. [Hair], [eyes], [skin], [one or two distinctive features], [what they wear at the collar: shirt, tie or ribbon, jacket].

**Crop and pose (copy the second image exactly):** square image, 1:1. The character **faces straight at the viewer**, centred, eyes looking directly into the lens. The **top of the hair just touches the top edge**. The **eyes sit about 30% down from the top** and the **chin about halfway down**. The frame ends at **mid-chest**, with the shirt collar and tie knot visible and the tie running off the bottom edge. One hand is raised with the **chin resting lightly on the curled fingers**, the hand coming up from the lower right. The shoulders are slightly cut off by the left and right edges.

**Ink aura (copy the second image's pattern, recoloured):**
- Ren's thick, marbled, liquid ink fills the **whole bottom third and sweeps up the right side to about shoulder height**. It flows in layered horizontal waves with **thin metallic veins** running through it, and **bleeds into the white shirt** as if the shirt is being painted.
- Use the same shape, flow and coverage, recoloured to **[signature colour + hex], [a darker shade] and black, with thin [silver / gold] veins**.
- [Optional theme touch at the edges, e.g. "the ink breaks into a few pixels", "a few petals", "a few raindrops"], but keep it mostly liquid and painterly, like the original.
- The top half of the background stays clean: **light textured grey with faint paint flecks**, as in the second image.

**Style:** soft painterly anime illustration, delicate linework, pale skin with a gentle blush, the same rendering as the second image.

No text, no watermark, a single square portrait only.

---

**If the result needs fixing:**
- **The face comes out like Ren's:** add "*a completely different person from the second image; same layout only*".
- **The theme touch takes over:** add "*the ink is mostly liquid marble, like the second image; only a little [theme] at the very edges*".
