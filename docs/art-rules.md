# Art rules

Rules for every piece of art made for Bizarre Chess: piece sets, effect sheets, portraits and wallpapers.

## No cross symbols on chess pieces

**Do not use a cross symbol on any chess piece design** (the classic cross on top of the king, or any cross shape anywhere on a piece).

- Kings get a different crown topper instead: an orb, a spire, a gem, flames, lightning, a ring, a jasmine bud… whatever fits the character.
- Add this line to every piece-set prompt: *"No cross symbol on any piece, including the king."*
- If a generated sheet comes back with a cross, erase it before it goes into the game (as was done for AI-0's king in `public/assets/pieces/wireframe/K.webp`).
