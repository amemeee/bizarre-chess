# KIV: characters to keep in view

Planned characters that aren't started yet. Nothing here is in the game.

## 1. The Homunculus

An artificial monster **created by Kyo and Shion**: grown in one of Shion's venom vials and bound in Kyo's iron chains. Innocent and empty like a child, but cold and monstrous at the board. A hidden boss.

- **Colours:** Shion's toxic lime green (#c6ff3d) and Kyo's crimson (#d1002c), twisting together without blending.
- **Look:** porcelain skin with a faint green tint and stitched seams; one lime eye, one crimson eye; ragged black hair with lime streaks; a cracked half-mask (a small copy of Kyo's); an oversized white shirt with a crooked tie; Kyo's chain on one wrist; a heart-shaped venom vial glowing in its chest.
- **Ultimate idea:** *Stitched*. It takes one of your captured pieces and brings it back on its own side, stitched together with chains and dripping venom.

### Design sheet prompt

Attach `kyo.jpg` and `shion.jpg`.

> Character design sheet for "Bizarre Chess", an anime chess-duel game. Match the art style of the attached reference portraits exactly: soft painterly anime illustration, delicate linework, expressive eyes, and swirling ink in the character's signature colours on a light textured grey background.
>
> **Character:** the Homunculus, an artificial being **created by the two people in the attached images**: Kyo, the masked crimson tyrant who founded the tournament, and Shion, his smiling lieutenant who brews poison. Appears about 15, genderless, unnervingly beautiful but clearly **not human**.
> **Personality:** innocent, curious and empty, like a child; it copies its creators' smiles without understanding them. At the chessboard it turns cold and monstrous.
> **Role in the tournament:** a hidden boss, a weapon grown to play perfect chess.
> **Signature colours:** **Shion's toxic lime green (#c6ff3d)** and **Kyo's deep crimson (#d1002c)** with black. The two colours swirl around it like poison and blood that never quite mix.
> **Theme / power:** grown in a glass vial. **Faint stitched seams** at the neck and wrists. **Kyo's black iron chains** wrapped loosely around one arm like restraints, a small padlock dangling. **Shion's glowing lime venom** seeping from the seams and dripping from its fingertips. A **heart-shaped glass vial** (like Shion's) **embedded in its chest**, glowing lime, half full.
> **Look:** pale, almost porcelain skin with a faint green tint under the surface; **heterochromia**: one **lime-green** eye (from Shion) and one **crimson** eye (from Kyo), with too-large pupils. **Short black hair** with uneven **lime-green streaks**, cut ragged. A **cracked half-mask over one side of its face**, a small copy of Kyo's. An **oversized white shirt** with a black tie tied wrong and sleeves too long, like borrowed clothes. Bare feet.
> **Holding:** a chess **pawn made of cloudy green glass, with venom sloshing inside it**.
>
> **Sheet layout, all on the same light grey background:**
> 1. Main portrait, square, chest-up, a three-quarter view, head tilted with a blank, curious smile, holding the glass pawn near its face, lime and crimson ink swirling around it.
> 2. Full-body standing pose, front view, slightly hunched, chains trailing on the floor, a small puddle of venom at its feet.
> 3. Five small expression heads: calm (blank), smug/happy (a smile that's slightly too wide), angry (monstrous, venom-green cracks spreading across its face), shocked, defeated (dissolving into venom).
> 4. Its glass pawn in close-up, plus its cracked half-mask and the heart vial.
>
> Eerie but beautiful, not gory. No text, no labels, no watermark.
